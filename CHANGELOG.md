# Changelog

All notable changes to the Nery Mizuki character repository.

- Created the canonical `nery` repository (GitHub, GitLab and YuGit mirrors) from the approved Mnerys mascot assets.
- License notice amended to **v1.1**: Section 6 authorizes distribution of the artwork by the rights holder through this official repository, and its use within Yuri Cunha's own properties (yuricunha.com and subdomains, Mnerys, Yuri Cunha's products). - Added the character identity and usage rules (`docs/IDENTITY.md`) and the artwork status document (`docs/ARTWORK-STATUS.md`), adapted from the Mnerys mascot documentation.

- Eight poses with real alpha, PNG and WebP exports.
- Avatars at 128/256/512 px, PNG and WebP.
