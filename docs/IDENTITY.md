# Nery Mizuki — identity and usage rules

## Who she is

Nery Mizuki is the friendly face of Mnerys and of Yuri Cunha's web properties: a cute, curious character in the Mizuki family. **She is a mascot, not a logo and not a chatbot persona.** The geometric M stays neutral across the UI, favicon, PWA and wordmark.

## Canonical look

- **Appearance:** copper-red hair, amber eyes and cream hair bows.
- **Clothes:** dark charcoal hoodie with warm terracotta/orange accents.
- **Illustration language:** friendly 2D anime/chibi style, dark charcoal backgrounds, warm highlights.

## Never do

- Change her hair or eye color.
- Use purple brand accents on her.
- Apply generic robot motifs.
- Replace the geometric M logo with a mascot-shaped icon (favicon, PWA, app icon or wordmark).
- Redraw or restyle her outside the approved character sheet without the rights holder's approval.
- Claim the artwork is open-source, AGPL-3.0 or freely reusable.
